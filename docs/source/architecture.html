1. What GrantLane is

GrantLane is a milestone grant escrow where a claim has to survive a challenge before it pays.

A grantor locks USDC against a list of milestones up front. When a milestone is done, the grantee publishes their evidence and claims it with a small USDC bond. The claim is open to dispute for a fixed window. If nobody disputes it, it pays out. If someone does, UMA decides, and whoever was wrong loses their bond.

No one approves a milestone by hand, and no key can release the escrowed money. The design does not try to judge whether work is good — nothing automated can. It makes lying expensive instead: the evidence is public, the claim is bonded, and anyone can challenge it.

The idea in one line: escrow the money, then let a bonded, publicly disputable claim release it.

2. User flow

The journey from an application to a completed grant, as the people involved experience it. It has two halves: getting funded happens once per grant, and getting paid repeats for every milestone.

2.1 Getting funded

Who does what

Applicant / granteeApplies, then claims each milestone with evidence and a bond, and withdraws what is approved. A Selfie Check gates applying, claiming and changing the payout wallet.
GrantorReviews applications, sets the milestone scope and escrows the grant. Usually the one who disputes a weak claim.
DisputerAnyone who believes a claim is false. They match the bond; if they are right, they take the grantee's bond.

SettlerAnyone — the grantee, a bystander, or the Chainlink CRE workflow. Settling applies an outcome; it never decides one.

4. A milestone's life

Every milestone moves through five states, all stored in GrantEscrow. The contract only lets it move along these arrows.

StateMeaningMoney
PendingFunded, not yet claimed.Held in escrow.
ClaimedAsserted on UMA; open to dispute until the window closes.Grantee's bond held by UMA.
DisputedChallenged; waiting for an answer.Both bonds held by UMA.
ApprovedSettled true.Amount credited to the payout wallet; bond returned (plus the disputer's, if any, less UMA's burn).
RejectedSettled false; can be claimed again.Grantee's bond goes to the disputer, less UMA's burn.

A grant is Completed once every milestone is Approved. The grantor can close a grant and reclaim what was never released, but not while any claim is Claimed or Disputed.

6. Proving a live human

World ID Selfie Check gates three moments: applying, claiming a milestone and changing the payout wallet. The last two end in a transaction, so the proof has to become something the contract can check. For a milestone claim it goes like this:

  • Each gate uses its own World ID action, so a proof obtained at one gate is worthless at another.
  • Tickets are HMAC-signed by the server, so an endpoint can tell a verified human from an invented identifier.
  • Attestations are EIP-712 signatures from the attestor key. GrantEscrow checks the signer, the deadline and a per-grant nonce, so a signature can't be replayed.
  • One open application per person: the World ID nullifier is stable per person, so switching wallets doesn't get around it.

7. Where data lives

The chain holds everything that decides money. The app store holds what people read, and hashes on-chain prove that it hasn't been changed.

WhereWhatWhy there
GrantEscrowGrant funder, grantee, payout wallet, totals, open claims, terms hash; each milestone's amount, status, dispute-window end, UMA assertion id, evidence hash; credited balances.These decide who is paid and when, so the contract is the authority.
UMAEach claim's text, asserter, bond, expiry, disputer and resolution.UMA owns dispute resolution; the escrow reads outcomes through callbacks.
App storeApplications (pitch, repo snapshot, milestone titles and criteria), evidence bundles keyed by their hash.Prose is too costly to store on-chain; the hashes make it tamper-evident.
The user's browserApplication form drafts, the short-lived verification ticket.Convenience only; never sent anywhere until submitted.

Two integrity checks tie the stores together. The server links an application to a grant only when its milestone set hashes to the grant's on-chain termsHash. The evidence page recomputes both the evidence hash and the terms hash and shows whether they match the chain, so nobody has to trust the site.

8. What enforces what

Rules the contract enforces

  • Only UMA resolves claims. The callbacks reject any other caller and ignore assertions the escrow didn't make.
  • Payouts are pulled, not pushed. Approved amounts are credited and withdrawn, so a failed transfer can never block settlement.
  • Settlement is permissionless. settle and performUpkeep work for anyone; performUpkeep re-checks every claim it is handed.
  • No closing under a live claim. closeGrant is refused while any milestone is Claimed or Disputed.
  • Terms are pinned. createGrant commits the terms hash, and every claim quotes it.
  • Claims need a live human. submitMilestone requires a valid attestation bound to the evidence hash and the grant's claim nonce.
